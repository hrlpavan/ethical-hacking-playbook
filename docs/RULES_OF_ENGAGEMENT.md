# 📜 Rules of Engagement & Legal Framework

Based on **Intigriti Researcher Terms & Conditions (Articles 1–18)** and international Safe Harbor standards.

---

## 1. Legal Authorization & Scope

* **Contractual Relationship**: Participating in a program creates a binding contractual relationship between the researcher and the sponsoring company. The researcher acts as an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or representative of Intigriti or the company.
* **Scope is Absolute**: Authorization to test applies **only** to assets explicitly designated as "In Scope" while the program is actively running. 
* **Criminal Liability for Scope Violations**: Testing out-of-scope assets or violating program conditions removes all authorization. Such actions can constitute illegal unauthorized access punishable under computer crime legislation (e.g., Article 550bis of the Belgian Criminal Code and corresponding cybercrime laws globally). Both the company and Intigriti reserve the right to initiate civil proceedings and file criminal complaints.

---

## 2. Strictly Prohibited Actions (Article 6)

When conducting security research, you must **NEVER**:

1. **Misuse Vulnerabilities**: Do not use any discovered weakness for extortion, personal gain, or unauthorized data collection.
2. **Over-Exploit**: Do not exploit more than necessary to verify the vulnerability and gather minimal evidence for your report.
3. **Degrade Availability (DoS / DDoS)**: Do not launch denial-of-service attacks, flood endpoints with volumetric traffic, or degrade service performance.
4. **Social Engineering & Physical Attacks**: Do not target company employees, support staff, contractors, or facilities with phishing or physical intrusion.
5. **Malware / Destructive Tools**: Do not deploy backdoors, web shells, botnet agents, or ransomware.
6. **Data Modification or Deletion**: Never alter, delete, or corrupt existing data or parameters. Testing data modifications is only permissible within accounts you own.
7. **Affect Third-Party Infrastructure**: If an out-of-scope third-party system is accidentally touched or affected, halt testing immediately and notify the company through the platform.

---

## 3. Obligation of Prompt Disclosure (Article 7)

* **Immediate Reporting**: Once a vulnerability is discovered, report it promptly through the Intigriti platform.
* **Good-Faith Collaboration**: Provide all necessary reproduction steps and respond constructively to triagers and security engineers seeking clarification.

---

## 4. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (Article 9)

* **Strict Non-Disclosure**: All company information, asset data, discovered vulnerabilities, and triage discussions are strictly confidential.
* **No Public Disclosure Without Mutual Consent**: You may **never** publish blog posts, tweets, videos, or public advisories without prior explicit written agreement from both the company and Intigriti.
* **Redaction Rights**: The company reserves the right to redact sensitive business information from any mutually agreed public disclosure.

---

## 5. Dispute Resolution & Mediation (Article 15)

* In the event of a dispute regarding scope, validity, or bounty payout:
  1. **Informal Phase**: Intigriti acts as an impartial coordinator to review evidence and reconcile positions.
  2. **Mediation Committee**: If unresolved within one month, Intigriti can convene a 5-member committee of independent security professionals.
  3. **Governing Law**: By default, agreements are governed by Belgian law and the jurisdiction of the Antwerp courts (division Antwerp), Belgium, unless otherwise stipulated in the program conditions.

---

## 6. Financial & Tax Compliance (Article 8 & 12)

* **Bounty Eligibility**: Bounties are awarded at the company's discretion for valid, novel (first to report), and verified vulnerabilities meeting severity thresholds.
* **Tax Responsibility**: Researchers are solely responsible for declaring bounty income, paying applicable personal/corporate income taxes, VAT, and social security contributions in their respective jurisdictions.
